BeamDeck: music from your car in BeamNG (and from everyone else's car on the server)
====================================================================================

You only do steps 1-4 once.

1. INSTALL THE TOOLS
   Open PowerShell and run:

       winget install Python.Python.3.12 Gyan.FFmpeg yt-dlp.yt-dlp

   (Use this Python, not the one from the Microsoft Store.)

2. INSTALL BEAMDECK
   Unzip this folder somewhere (e.g. Documents\BeamDeck), open PowerShell in it
   (Shift + right-click in the folder > "Open PowerShell window here") and run:

       powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install.ps1

   "BeamDeck" now appears in your Start menu.

3. TAILSCALE
   Be connected to Tailscale (the same way you join the BeamMP server).

4. CONNECT TO THE MUSIC RELAY
   Open BeamDeck from the Start menu > Settings tab > Multiplayer.
   Music relay address:

       {{RELAY}}

   (Typing just 100.x.x.x:47140 is fine too.) Press Save, then Test: it should
   say "Connected · N players sharing now".


EVERY TIME YOU PLAY
-------------------
* Open BeamDeck first and leave its window open: the sound comes out of it.
  Click anywhere in it once so it's allowed to play sound.
* Join the BeamMP server. It downloads the BeamDeck game mod by itself.
* Play your own music in BeamDeck: paste a YouTube link in the Play tab, or use
  the Music tab (your own music folders; set them in Settings).
* Your music plays from your car; you hear everyone else's music from their
  cars (muffled from outside, louder close by, with Doppler when they pass).
* Car tab > "Share my music" / "Share my screen" turn sharing off if you want them private.
* TV prop: spawn the TV with its "BeamDeck" configuration and it shows your car screen; near it,
  the music plays from the TV.
* With Car screens on, everyone sees your car screen (video, Android Auto, now playing) on your car.
* Voice chat: players near you hear you from where you are (your car or on foot), quieter
  with distance. The first time, BeamDeck asks for the microphone: allow it. Use headphones.
  Car tab > Voice chat / Mute mic; Settings > Multiplayer > push to talk, microphone, sensitivity.
* Game controls (BeamNG Options > Controls, search "BeamDeck"): play / pause, next and
  previous song, push to talk, mute mic,
  music volume up/down, voice volume up/down. Bind the keys you want there.

Tips
* If YouTube links stop working: run  yt-dlp -U  in PowerShell.
* Doors open or windows broken let the music out of a car.
* Car tab > Car screens puts your video on the car's dashboard screen.


OPTIONAL: ANDROID AUTO ON THE CAR SCREEN (Android phone + USB cable)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Once:
1. Reinstall with Google's head unit included:
       powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install.ps1 -AndroidAuto
2. Plug the phone in by USB, open BeamDeck > Car tab > Android Auto (the phone
   switches into "car mode"). Leave it plugged in.
3. Windows gives the phone the wrong driver in car mode ("USB Composite Device"),
   so the head unit can't attach. Fix it once with Zadig (https://zadig.akeo.ie):
     - In PowerShell:  adb kill-server
     - Turn on BeamDeck > Car tab > Android Auto (the phone switches to car mode;
       it only stays there while the head unit keeps trying, so be quick).
     - Zadig > Options > List All Devices, untick "Ignore Hubs or Composite Parents".
     - Pick the phone's entry whose USB ID is  18D1 2D01  that is the COMPOSITE
       DEVICE itself (no "(Interface 0/1)" in the name; its current driver shows
       usbccgp), choose WinUSB, press Replace Driver.
     - Unplug, replug, turn Android Auto off and on in BeamDeck.
   Signs you need this: BeamDeck / the head unit keeps saying "Attaching to USB
   device..." / "Couldn't find/access compatible USB device" or "Plug the phone in".
   Normal phone USB / file transfer is not affected (that's a different USB ID).
   Undo: Device Manager > the device under "Universal Serial Bus devices" >
   Uninstall device, tick "Attempt to remove the driver", replug.
4. On the phone: Android Auto settings > Previously connected cars >
   menu > "Add new cars to Android Auto" must be ticked.
5. In BeamNG, sit in a car with a screen, then BeamDeck > Car tab > Set up screen:
   click the screen's 4 corners in the game, move the camera a little (W/S), click
   the 4 corners again. After that, clicking the car's screen taps Android Auto.

Every time:
* Plug the phone in, then BeamDeck > Car tab > Android Auto.
* A window called "Android Auto - Desktop Head Unit" opens: keep it visible
  somewhere on screen (a second monitor or a corner is fine), because BeamDeck
  copies its picture. It doesn't need to be in front.
* Android Auto's own sound (music, navigation voice) needs VB-CABLE
  (https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) and NirSoft's svcl.exe
  (https://www.nirsoft.net/utils/sound_volume_command_line.html) copied into
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamDeck\app . Without them the picture works but it's silent.
* Car tab > Game map puts BeamNG's own map over Google Maps.


OPTIONAL: WIRELESS ANDROID AUTO (no cable; phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once:
1. In PowerShell:   winget install Google.PlatformTools
   then RESTART THE PC (so BeamDeck finds adb).
2. Install BeamDeck with the head unit:  install.ps1 -AndroidAuto  (see above).
3. Phone: Settings > About phone > tap "Build number" 7 times (Developer options).
4. Phone: Settings > System > Developer options > Wireless debugging: ON >
   "Pair device with pairing code". On the PC run (with the numbers it shows):
       adb pair 192.168.x.x:PAIRPORT        and type the 6-digit code.
5. Phone: Android Auto settings > tap "Version" 10 times (developer mode).
6. BeamDeck > Settings > Android Auto > Phone connection: "Head unit server" > Save.

Every time:
1. Phone: Wireless debugging ON; look at "IP address & port" on its screen
   (the port changes each time).
2. PC:   adb connect 192.168.x.x:PORT
3. Phone: Android Auto settings > menu (three dots) > Start head unit server.
4. BeamDeck > Car tab > Android Auto.
If BeamDeck says something is missing, it tells you what (head unit, adb, phone).
